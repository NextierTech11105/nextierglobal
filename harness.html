Five execution contracts

The Wizard's answers fill five coordinated contracts.

01

Data Contract

Who qualifies, what signals matter, when data is refreshed, and what makes someone reachable.

02

Pursuit Contract

Channel, cadence, message, block size, authorization and volume mandate.

03

Digital Workforce Contract

What AI detects, prepares, classifies, recommends, and executes within approved boundaries.

04

Human Execution Contract

Who takes conversations, how competency is demonstrated, who decides advancement, and who follows through.

05

Outcome Contract

What counts as completion, where responsibility transfers, and how origination is measured.

Governance at the center

The default campaign boundary is a 15-minute Discovery, booked and confirmed completed by a person. Qualified opportunities then move to an accountable deal owner.

Architectural rule. The Wizard changes the configuration, not the engine. Every workflow stays reusable, remixable, measurable, reversible where appropriate, and governed by explicit human and AI responsibilities.
