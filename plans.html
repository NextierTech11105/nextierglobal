WhyExecution can be assisted. Trust, judgment and accountability stay human.

Governance boundary. AI can classify, prioritize, recommend, prepare, and execute authorized routines. Only humans make consequential lead-advancement decisions. Every action must be attributable, logged, and subject to permissions, quiet hours, STOP/DNC controls, and exception handling.
