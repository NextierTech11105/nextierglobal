Questions

What does Nextier do for real estate deal makers?

It turns property data into conversations: every lot and owner in your territory, the reason to call now, and the owner's cell, ranked.

Where does the data come from?

Public records and your own accounts. New York loads from open records today. Other states run on a property data plan in your name.

Does anything send on its own?

No. Nothing goes out until you press Send. Stop and do-not-call numbers are blocked.
