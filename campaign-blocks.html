Who takes the next move?

Digital Workforce prepares, tags, classifies, and routes. The Campaign Director sets the mandate, cadence, and capacity. The Campaign Manager works the queue. The advisor takes the conversation and decides advancement.

Consent, STOP/DNC, quiet hours, and capacity checks govern each send. Eligible follow-up returns to the Pursuit Engine. Booked Discovery and human-confirmed completion are recorded separately.
