MCP

Plug the Data Engine straight into Claude, Cursor, Windsurf or any client that speaks the Model Context Protocol. Your AI can count, search, enrich and check your queues in plain English, against the same workspace as the app.

  • Reads first. 7 of the 11 tools only read.
  • Enrichment is billed, and says so. The 4 enrichment tools write results back to your leads and are metered per use.
  • Nothing sends. No tool texts, calls or emails anyone.
  • Same truth as the app. Counts come from the same tables the operators work from.

Status

The server runs today for Nextier operators. Client access, signing in with your own Data Engine key and pinned to your workspace, is the next build. Request access.

What to ask

"How many contactable pre-foreclosure leads do we have in Suffolk?"
"Show me every attempt on this lead and whether it was delivered."
"Which campaigns are paused, and how many leads are left in each?"
"How many leads are waiting on the call queue right now?"

MCP or API?

Use the API

When a system does the work: pipelines, schedulers, your CRM, a data warehouse. It reads, tags and stages.

Use the MCP

When a person asks the questions through an AI assistant.
