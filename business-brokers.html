Questions

What does Nextier do for business brokers?

It turns business data into owners you can call: your market, the owner's direct line, and a call button on every record.

Is it texting or calling?

Calling first. For business owners, Nextier is set up call-first, from your own line.

What happens when an owner calls back?

The callback goes to the top of your list, with what they said.
